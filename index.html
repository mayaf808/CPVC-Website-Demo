01 Influencers

Key creators shaping opinions, aesthetics, and purchasing habits today.

Creator Economy

Micro-Expert Creators

Audiences are shifting trust away from lifestyle mega-influencers toward niche experts who provide hyper-focused knowledge in cooking, tech, and finance.

Marketing Opportunity Partner with creators who have smaller, highly engaged audiences to build authentic product trust.
Format Shift

Raw Vlog Aesthetics

Polished studio lighting is out. Unedited, conversational direct-to-camera videos are driving higher conversion rates across Gen Z and Millennials.

Marketing Opportunity Drop heavy production values in social ads in favor of mobile-shot, authentic commentary.

02 National Events

Major cultural gatherings, sports events, and shared public experiences.

Sports & Entertainment

Halftime Show Virality

National championship halftime performances are generating more real-time meme discourse than the sports games themselves across X and TikTok.

Marketing Opportunity Prepare real-time social media response templates to participate in live event discourse instantly.
Experiential

Convention Culture Boom

Comic conventions and gaming expos are seeing record attendance from Gen X parents attending alongside their Gen Z children.

Marketing Opportunity Design multi-generational booth activations that appeal to both nostalgia and modern fandoms.

03 Pop Culture

Film, television, aesthetic movements, and shared cross-generational phenomena.

Film & TV

Retro Sci-Fi Aesthetics

Neon lighting, dystopian themes, and 1980s synth visuals continue to dominate cinema box offices and streaming queues.

Marketing Opportunity Incorporate retro-futuristic design cues into upcoming digital campaigns to capture immediate visual attention.
Fashion & Lifestyle

Gorpcore Streetwear

Outdoor utility wear like hiking boots, technical jackets, and cargo pants remain a staple in casual urban fashion across all age demographics.

Marketing Opportunity Highlight durability and utility in product photography, even for everyday consumer goods.

04 Social Media Trends

Algorithmic audio, viral challenges, and platform shifts.

Platform Dynamics

Search Engine Migration

Younger demographics are using short-form video platforms as primary search engines for product reviews, travel tips, and recipes.

Marketing Opportunity Optimize video captions with descriptive keywords rather than hashtags for better search discoverability.
Audio Trends

Fast-Tempo Remixes

Speeding up classical or 1990s tracks by 150 percent creates instantly recognizable audio clips that boost algorithm reach.

Marketing Opportunity License upbeat or sped-up audio tracks for short-form video campaigns to increase viewer retention.

05 Music

Genre blends, global sounds, and streaming list movements.

Genre Fusion

Country Pop Crossovers

Traditional country instrumentation combined with modern pop beats is topping global playlists, attracting both Gen Z and Gen X listeners.

Marketing Opportunity Utilize acoustic-electric hybrid soundtracks in video advertising for broad demographic appeal.
Global Sounds

Afrobeats Dominance

Rhythmic Afrobeats tracks are dominating summer charts and festival lineups, securing massive crossover popularity in North America and Europe.

Marketing Opportunity Incorporate energetic, global rhythmic audio to give commercial spots a modern, upbeat feel.
